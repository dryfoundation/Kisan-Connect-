# Kisan-Connect-
KisanConnect — a smart agricultural marketplace connecting farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers, with AI-powered market insights, logistics, reviews, and unsold-produce management.
🌱 KisanConnect

From Farm to You

KisanConnect is a smart agricultural e-commerce platform designed to connect farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers while providing market insights, logistics support, transparent pricing, product reviews, and tools for managing unsold produce.

Key Features
👨‍🌾 Farmer Marketplace — Farmers can create and manage produce listings.
🛒 Buyer Marketplace — Consumers and bulk buyers can browse and purchase fresh produce.
📊 AI Market Advisor — Provides market and pricing insights to assist farmers.
🚚 Logistics Tracking — Tracks orders and delivery information.
📸 Real Crop Photos — Farmers can upload actual photographs of their produce.
⭐ Consumer Reviews — Buyers can rate products, leave comments, and upload review photos.
🛡️ Trust & Verification — Farmer/listing verification and buyer-protection information.
⚠️ Unsold Stock Management — Helps identify and manage produce at risk of remaining unsold.
🏢 Admin Dashboard — Provides tools for farmer verification, listing moderation, and dispute/risk management.
📱 Responsive Interface — Designed for both desktop and mobile use.
